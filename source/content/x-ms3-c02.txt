## 2.2
@ex Sum formula
Q: Prove $1+3+5+\dots+(2n-1)=n^2$.
S: Basis: $n=1$: $1=1^2$.
S: Assume true for $n=k$: sum $=k^2$.
S: Then $k^2+(2k+1)=(k+1)^2$.
A: True for all $n\in\mathbb N$ by induction.
@p **Layout of every induction proof.** (1) Statement $P(n)$. (2) Basis: show $P(1)$ true. (3) Assumption: assume $P(k)$ true. (4) Inductive step: use it to prove $P(k+1)$. (5) Conclusion sentence. Marks are allocated to each of these labelled parts.
## 2.3
@ex Divisibility
Q: Prove $5^n-1$ is divisible by $4$ for $n\ge1$.
S: Basis: $5-1=4$.
S: Assume $5^k-1=4m$.
S: $5^{k+1}-1=5(5^k)-1=5(4m+1)-1=20m+4=4(5m+1)$.
A: Divisible by 4 for all $n\ge1$.
## 2.4
@ex Double angle from De Moivre
Q: Use $(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^2$ to derive $\cos2\theta$.
S: Expand: $\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta+2i\sin\theta\cos\theta$.
S: De Moivre says it equals $\cos2\theta+i\sin2\theta$. Equate real parts.
A: $\cos2\theta=\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta$
