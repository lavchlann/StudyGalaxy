## 3.2
@ex Magnitude and unit vector
Q: $\mathbf a=2\mathbf i-2\mathbf j+\mathbf k$. Find $|\mathbf a|$ and $\hat{\mathbf a}$.
S: $|\mathbf a|=\sqrt{4+4+1}=3$.
A: $3$ and $\frac13(2\mathbf i-2\mathbf j+\mathbf k)$
## 3.3
@ex Angle
Q: Angle between $\mathbf a=(1,2,2)$ and $\mathbf b=(2,0,1)$.
S: $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=2+0+2=4$; $|\mathbf a|=3$; $|\mathbf b|=\sqrt5$.
S: $\cos\theta=\frac4{3\sqrt5}$, $\theta\approx0.93$ rad ($53.4^\circ$).
A: about $53.4^\circ$
@ex Perpendicular
Q: Find $t$ so that $(t,2,-1)\perp(3,1,t)$.
S: Dot product $=3t+2-t=2t+2=0$.
A: $t=-1$
## 3.4
@ex Collinear points
Q: Show $A(1,2,3)$, $B(3,6,7)$, $C(4,8,9)$ are collinear.
S: $\vec{AB}=(2,4,4)$, $\vec{AC}=(3,6,6)$.
S: $\vec{AC}=\frac32\vec{AB}$, so they are parallel and share $A$.
A: Collinear.
## 3.5
@ex Line through two points
Q: Vector equation of the line through $(1,0,2)$ and $(3,2,1)$.
S: Direction $(2,2,-1)$.
A: $\mathbf r=(1,0,2)+\lambda(2,2,-1)$
@ex Intersection
Q: Do $\mathbf r=(0,0,0)+s(1,1,1)$ and $\mathbf r=(1,0,0)+t(0,1,1)$ intersect?
S: $s=1$ (x), $s=t$ (y), $s=t$ (z): $t=1$, consistent.
A: Yes, at $(1,1,1)$.
## 3.6
@ex Cross product
Q: $(1,2,3)\times(0,1,4)$.
S: $(2\cdot4-3\cdot1,\ 3\cdot0-1\cdot4,\ 1\cdot1-2\cdot0)$.
A: $(5,\,-4,\,1)$
@ex Triangle area
Q: Area of the triangle with $\vec{AB}=(1,1,0)$, $\vec{AC}=(0,1,1)$.
S: Cross $=(1,-1,1)$, magnitude $\sqrt3$.
A: $\frac{\sqrt3}2$
## 3.7
@ex Plane
Q: Plane through $(1,0,0)$ with normal $(2,1,-1)$.
S: $2x+y-z=2$.
A: $2x+y-z=2$
