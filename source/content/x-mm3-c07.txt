## 7.2
@p **Bernoulli conditions:** one trial, exactly two outcomes (success or failure), fixed success probability $p$. Then $E(X)=p$ and $\text{Var}(X)=p(1-p)$.
@ex Bernoulli
Q: A free throw succeeds with $p=0.7$. State $E(X)$ and $\text{Var}(X)$.
S: $E=0.7$; $\text{Var}=0.7\times0.3=0.21$.
A: $0.7$ and $0.21$
## 7.3
@ex Binomial probabilities
Q: $X\sim\text{Bin}(5,0.4)$. Find $P(X=2)$ and $P(X\ge1)$.
S: $P(X=2)=\binom52(0.4)^2(0.6)^3=10(0.16)(0.216)=0.3456$.
S: $P(X\ge1)=1-0.6^5=1-0.07776=0.92224$.
A: $0.3456$ and $0.9222$
@warn Always justify a binomial model: fixed number of independent trials, two outcomes, constant $p$. Sampling without replacement from a small group breaks independence.
## 7.4
@ex Mean and standard deviation
Q: $X\sim\text{Bin}(20,0.25)$. Find mean, variance and sd.
S: $np=5$; $np(1-p)=3.75$; $\sqrt{3.75}\approx1.936$.
A: $5$, $3.75$, $1.94$
## 7.5
@ex Modelling
Q: 8% of parts are faulty. In a batch of 12, find the probability that at most one is faulty.
S: $P(0)=0.92^{12}=0.3677$; $P(1)=12(0.08)(0.92)^{11}=0.3837$.
S: Sum $=0.7514$.
A: about $0.751$
@ex Smallest n
Q: Find the least $n$ so that $P(\text{at least one success})>0.9$ with $p=0.2$.
S: $1-0.8^n>0.9\Rightarrow0.8^n<0.1\Rightarrow n>\frac{\ln0.1}{\ln0.8}\approx10.3$.
A: $n=11$

@p **Phrases to convert.** "At least 2" is $1-P(X\le1)$. "More than 2" is $1-P(X\le2)$. "Fewer than 4" is $P(X\le3)$. "Between 2 and 5 inclusive" is $P(X\le5)-P(X\le1)$.
