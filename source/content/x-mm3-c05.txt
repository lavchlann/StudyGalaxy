## 5.2
@ex Splitting terms
Q: Find $\int\left(3x^2-\frac4{x^3}+\sqrt x\right)dx$.
S: Rewrite: $3x^2-4x^{-3}+x^{1/2}$.
S: Integrate each: $x^3+2x^{-2}+\frac23x^{3/2}+c$.
A: $x^3+\dfrac2{x^2}+\dfrac23x^{3/2}+c$
@ex Finding $c$
Q: $f'(x)=6x-2$ and $f(1)=5$. Find $f$.
S: $f=3x^2-2x+c$; $f(1)=1+c=5\Rightarrow c=4$.
A: $f(x)=3x^2-2x+4$
## 5.3
@ex Linear inside
Q: $\int\left(e^{3x}+e^{-x}\right)dx$.
S: $\frac13e^{3x}-e^{-x}+c$.
A: $\frac13e^{3x}-e^{-x}+c$
## 5.4
@ex Reverse chain rule
Q: $\int\dfrac{2x}{x^2+1}dx$.
S: Numerator is the derivative of the denominator.
A: $\ln(x^2+1)+c$
@ex $\frac{1}{ax+b}$
Q: $\int\dfrac{5}{2x+3}dx$.
S: $\frac52\ln|2x+3|+c$ (divide by the inner derivative 2).
A: $\frac52\ln|2x+3|+c$
## 5.5
@ex Trig mix
Q: $\int(\sin 2x+4\cos x)dx$.
S: $-\frac12\cos2x+4\sin x+c$.
A: $-\frac12\cos 2x+4\sin x+c$
## 5.6
@ex Area between curves
Q: Area between $y=x$ and $y=x^2$.
S: Intersect at $x=0,1$; line is above.
S: $\int_0^1(x-x^2)dx=\left[\frac{x^2}2-\frac{x^3}3\right]_0^1=\frac16$.
A: $\frac16$
@int riemann | Add rectangles and watch the estimate close in on the exact integral. A definite integral is the limit of such sums.
@warn For area, $\int_a^b f$ gives *signed* area. If the graph crosses the axis, split at the crossing and add the absolute values.
## 5.7
@ex Velocity to position
Q: $v=3t^2-4$, $x(0)=2$. Find $x(t)$ and the displacement over $0\le t\le2$.
S: $x=t^3-4t+c$, $c=2$.
S: Displacement $=x(2)-x(0)=(8-8+2)-2=0$.
A: $x=t^3-4t+2$; displacement $0$.
## X | Exam toolkit
@p **Check by differentiating.** Every antiderivative can be verified in seconds. Do it whenever you have spare time. If you differentiate your answer and do not recover the integrand, find the mistake.
@p **Presentation.** Indefinite integrals need $+c$. Definite integrals show the bracket $[\;]_a^b$ and both substitutions. When a question says "exact", leave $e$ and $\ln$ unevaluated.
@quiz Power | Find $\int\left(4x^3-6x\right)dx$.
A: x^4-3x^2+c
AD: $x^4-3x^2+c$
SOL: Raise power and divide.
H: $x^{n+1}/(n+1)$.||Each term.||Add $c$.
@quiz Exponential | $\int 5e^{5x}dx$.
A: e^(5x)+c
AD: $e^{5x}+c$
SOL: Divide by 5.
H: Inner derivative 5.||$5\div5=1$.||Add $c$.
@quiz Log | $\int\frac{3}{x}dx$ for $x>0$.
A: 3lnx+c
ALT: 3ln(x)+c
AD: $3\ln x+c$
SOL: $\int\frac1x=\ln x$.
H: Constant multiple.||$\ln x$.||Add $c$.
@quiz Reverse chain | $\int\frac{e^x}{e^x+1}dx$.
A: ln(e^x+1)+c
AD: $\ln(e^x+1)+c$
SOL: Numerator is the derivative of the denominator.
H: $f'/f$.||$f=e^x+1$.||Add $c$.
@quiz Definite | $\int_0^1 3x^2dx$.
A: 1
AD: $1$
SOL: $[x^3]_0^1$.
H: Antiderivative.||$x^3$.||Substitute.
@quiz Definite | $\int_0^{\pi}\sin x\,dx$.
A: 2
AD: $2$
SOL: $[-\cos x]_0^\pi=1+1$.
H: $-\cos x$.||At $\pi$: $1$.||At $0$: $-1$.
@quiz Area | Area under $y=e^x$ from $0$ to $\ln3$.
A: 2
AD: $2$
SOL: $[e^x]_0^{\ln3}=3-1$.
H: Antiderivative.||$e^{\ln3}=3$.||Subtract.
@quiz Initial value | $f'(x)=4x$, $f(0)=3$. Find $f(2)$.
A: 11
AD: $11$
SOL: $f=2x^2+3$, $f(2)=11$.
H: Integrate.||$c=3$.||Substitute.
@quiz Between curves | Area between $y=x$ and $y=x^2$ from $0$ to $1$ (exact).
A: 1/6
AD: $\frac16$
SOL: $\int_0^1(x-x^2)dx$.
H: Which is on top?||Integrate difference.||Evaluate.
@quiz Velocity | $v=2t$, $x(0)=1$. Find $x(3)$.
A: 10
AD: $10$
SOL: $x=t^2+1$, $x(3)=10$.
H: Integrate.||$c=1$.||Substitute.
@comet Extension: Average value | The average value of $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,3]$ is?
A: 3
AD: $3$
SOL: $\frac1{3}\int_0^3x^2dx=\frac13\cdot9=3$.
H: $\frac1{b-a}\int f$.||$\int_0^3x^2=9$.||Divide by 3.
