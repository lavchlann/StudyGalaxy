## 4.2
@ex Reading derivatives from a graph
Q: The graph of $y=f(x)$ has a maximum at $x=2$ and crosses the $x$-axis at $x=5$. Describe $f'$ and $f''$ at $x=2$.
S: At a maximum the tangent is flat: $f'(2)=0$.
S: The curve bends down: $f''(2)<0$.
A: $f'(2)=0$ and $f''(2)<0$.
## 4.3
@ex Inflection of a cubic
Q: Find the point of inflection of $f(x)=x^3-6x^2+9x$.
S: $f'=3x^2-12x+9$, $f''=6x-12=0\Rightarrow x=2$.
S: $f''$ changes sign (negative to positive) so it is a genuine inflection; $f(2)=2$.
A: $(2,\ 2)$
@warn $f''(a)=0$ is necessary but not sufficient. For $y=x^4$, $f''(0)=0$ but there is no inflection (it is a minimum). Always check the sign change.
## 4.4
@ex Full sketch
Q: Sketch $y=x^3-3x^2$: intercepts, stationary points, inflection.
S: Intercepts: $x^2(x-3)=0\Rightarrow x=0,3$; $y$-intercept $0$.
S: $y'=3x^2-6x=0\Rightarrow x=0,2$; $y''=6x-6$: $y''(0)=-6$ max at $(0,0)$; $y''(2)=6$ min at $(2,-4)$.
S: Inflection at $x=1$, $y=-2$.
A: Max $(0,0)$, min $(2,-4)$, inflection $(1,-2)$, $x$-intercepts $0$ and $3$.
## 4.5
@ex Open box
Q: A box has square base side $x$ and no lid. Volume $32$. Find $x$ minimising the surface area.
S: Height $h=\frac{32}{x^2}$; $S=x^2+4xh=x^2+\frac{128}{x}$.
S: $S'=2x-\frac{128}{x^2}=0\Rightarrow x^3=64\Rightarrow x=4$; $S''>0$ so minimum.
A: $x=4$, giving $S=48$.
@p **Optimisation recipe.** (1) Draw and name variables. (2) Write the quantity to optimise. (3) Use the constraint to eliminate to one variable. (4) Differentiate, solve. (5) Prove max/min with $f''$ or a sign table. (6) Check the end points of the domain. (7) Answer the question with units.
## X | Exam toolkit
@p **Endpoints.** On a closed interval $[a,b]$ the absolute maximum may be at an end point. List: end points, stationary points, and any non-differentiable points, then compare function values.
@p **Describing graphs.** Use full sentences with evidence: "$f''(x)>0$ for $x>1$, so the graph is concave up there". Mentioning only the sign of $f'$ for concavity is a classic lost mark.
