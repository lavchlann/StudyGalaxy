## 5.3
@ex Gaussian elimination
Q: $x+y=3$, $2x-y=0$.
S: Add the equations: $3x=3\Rightarrow x=1$, then $y=2$.
A: $x=1,\ y=2$
## 5.4
@ex Parameter
Q: For which $k$ does $x+y=1$, $2x+2y=k$ have no solution?
S: Parallel lines unless $k=2$.
A: $k\ne2$
## 5.5
@ex Leslie matrix
Q: $L=\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\0.5&0\end{pmatrix}$, population $(10,\ 6)$ next generation.
S: $(0\cdot10+2\cdot6,\ 0.5\cdot10+0)=(12,\ 5)$.
A: $(12,\ 5)$
## 5.6
@ex Steady state
Q: Transition matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0.9&0.2\\0.1&0.8\end{pmatrix}$. Find the steady state.
S: $0.1a=0.2b\Rightarrow a=2b$; with $a+b=1$, $b=\frac13$.
A: $\left(\frac23,\ \frac13\right)$
