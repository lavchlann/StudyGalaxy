## 4.2
@ex Circle parametric
Q: Convert $\mathbf r=(3\cos t,\ 3\sin t)$ to Cartesian.
S: $x^2+y^2=9\cos^2t+9\sin^2t=9$.
A: $x^2+y^2=9$
## 4.3
@ex Collision
Q: $\mathbf r_1=(t,\,2t)$ and $\mathbf r_2=(4-t,\,3)$. Do they meet?
S: $t=4-t\Rightarrow t=2$ and $2t=3\Rightarrow t=1.5$.
S: Times differ, so the paths cross but the objects do not collide.
A: No collision.
## 4.4
@ex Differentiate
Q: $\mathbf r=(t^2,\ e^{t})$. Find $\dot{\mathbf r}$ and $\ddot{\mathbf r}$.
S: $\dot{\mathbf r}=(2t,e^t)$; $\ddot{\mathbf r}=(2,e^t)$.
A: As above.
@ex Integrate
Q: $\mathbf v=(2t,\,3)$, $\mathbf r(0)=(1,0)$.
S: $\mathbf r=(t^2+1,\ 3t)$.
A: $\mathbf r=(t^2+1,\ 3t)$
## 4.5
@ex Speed
Q: Speed at $t=1$ for $\mathbf r=(t^2,\ t^3)$.
S: $\dot{\mathbf r}=(2t,3t^2)=(2,3)$.
S: Speed $=\sqrt{13}$.
A: $\sqrt{13}$
## 4.6
@ex Projectile
Q: Launch at $20$ m/s at $30^\circ$. Position $(20\cos30^\circ\,t,\ 20\sin30^\circ\,t-5t^2)$. When does it land?
S: $10t-5t^2=0\Rightarrow t=2$.
A: $t=2$ s
