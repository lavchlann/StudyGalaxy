# Study Galaxy

A personal study guide for QCE Math Methods, Math Specialist and Physics, drawn as a night sky of constellations: lessons, quizzes, flashcards, formula sheets, an exam planner, achievements and more.

**Open it:** https://lavchlann.github.io/StudyGalaxy/

- Progress is saved in your own browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- "Ask Claude", written lessons and answer marking use your own Anthropic API key, pasted into Settings. The key stays in your browser and is sent only to Anthropic.
- When a new version is deployed, an **Update ready** button appears. Press it when you are ready to refresh.
- Settings has a backup download and restore.

Formula sheets are transcribed from the QCAA 2025 formula books. © State of Queensland (QCAA) 2025, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Source and build scripts are in `source/`.
